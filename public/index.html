FAQ

Questions

How much does a repair cost?

Plumbing starts from 9,000 ₸ and electrical work from 5,000 ₸. For other categories the specialist names a fixed price after seeing a description or photo, always before the visit.

How do you check specialists?

Every specialist fills in an application. Our team checks identity and experience before the first order, and customer ratings decide who gets priority.

What does the 30-day guarantee cover?

If a problem appears within 30 days of the job, report it on the “My requests” page. Our team reviews it and arranges corrective work with the specialist or another checked specialist.

Do I need to register?

No. You only need your phone number to send a request and to track it.

Where do you work?

We are piloting in Astana. RepairHub is a student project of the Technological Entrepreneurship course, and requests are handled by our team.
